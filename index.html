About Direct GovIndia

Direct GovIndia is an independent public-service directory built to help Indian citizens find official government websites without getting lost in confusing menus or misleading search ads. Every link on this site points straight to verified central and state government portals (*.gov.in / *.nic.in). We do not collect any personal data, host third-party forms, or charge any fees.
